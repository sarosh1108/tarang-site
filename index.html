About Tarang Offshore Dynamics

Tarang Offshore Dynamics is an independent consultancy based in Kochi, India, specializing in advanced offshore oil & gas engineering. We combine deep technical expertise with practical offshore experience to deliver safe, efficient, and innovative solutions for floating systems, moorings, risers, and sub-sea infrastructure.

Our Core Services

Mooring System Design & Analysis

Spread mooring, turret mooring, dynamic positioning studies, and extreme weather analysis.

Hydrodynamic Analysis

Wave, current & wind load calculations using OrcaFlex, AQWA, WAMIT, and in-house tools.

Naval Architecture & Stability

FPSO, FSO, FLNG, semi-submersibles, jack-ups — intact & damage stability compliance.
